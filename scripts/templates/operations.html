    Dark-status labels (paper and release)

    These labels describe same-day AIS proximity, without identifying the visible hulls or determining legality.

    FULLY_DARK
    No AIS vessels within the search radius that day.
    PARTIALLY_DARK
    One AIS vessel within the radius that day.
    AIS_PRESENT
    Two or more AIS vessels within the radius that day.
    DETECTED_ONLY
    No AIS supplied. The release stores AIS_MISSING and leaves vessel presence unknown.
    AIS_ERROR
    AIS verification failed. The release distinguishes AIS_MISSING (unavailable daily records) and AIS_INVALID (invalid records).
    NO_STS
    A completed scan has no STS candidates; its detection export is empty.
